(01) — The Tracks

Choose your starting point. We’ll do the rest.

Three condition-focused tracks. Each one is personalised after your assessment.

Prevent

Stay ahead of the joints and bones you’ll need at 70.

For early signs, family history, or age-related risk. Build strength and bone density before problems start.

Manage

Work around the pain. Then work it down.

For those living with arthritis, joint pain, or disc issues. Reduce flare-ups and build resilience.

Strengthen

Rebuild real strength in your bones and joints — safely, with oversight.

For severe degeneration, low bone density, or rebuilding after a setback. Rebuild real strength safely under close medical guidance.

(06) — What to expect

The 16-week shape.

Week 101

Assessment & baseline

Medical intake done. Pain mapped. A program built for your body — not a generic template.

Weeks 4–602

Pain quietening

Supporting muscles starting to switch back on. Most members notice the first meaningful drop in daily pain.

Month 203

Muscles activating — start loading

Now the body can take load. Standing strength, real-life patterns, full-body work that protects the joint.

Month 304

Resilience & routine

Higher capacity, fewer flare-ups, a routine you can carry on your own. Medical reassessment to confirm.
